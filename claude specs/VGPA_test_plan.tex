\section*{Why these tests}
VGPA is label-free view-graph pose averaging from the same lab: it beats RESfM on most
scenes under its own lenses, but every one of its numbers is a single run, in the
published-RESfM environment (its main-table RESfM column is the paper's 0.169 draw ---
verified by recomputing their column means), on its own track preprocessing, and never
under heavy contamination, never on Olsson, never at 60\%. Each test below closes one of
those gaps. Ordered by cost and by decision value; every analysis lands in the
already-built five-lens machinery (\code{compute\_vgpa\_metrics.py} +
\code{analyze\_vgpa\_style.py}, \code{FIVELENS\_readout.pdf}).

\section*{Tier 1 --- cheap, immediately publishable (days)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=4pt]
\item \textbf{Released checkpoint, our environment, repeated runs.} Their pipeline
includes test-time fine-tuning and robust BA --- both nondeterminism sources we have
quantified for our own arms (TTT $\sim$0.14 mean-level; BA floor $\mathcal{O}(0.05)$).
Three-plus repeats of their released model on their benchmarks measures \emph{their}
run-to-run variance. Their appendix already concedes BA nondeterminism; this puts a
number on it. \emph{Decision value: extends the environment-noise finding to a second
method; rebuttal ammunition regardless of outcome.}
\item \textbf{Five-lens readout of their released model on their tracks.} Per-scene
wins, mean-vs-median with blow-up attribution (their MegaDepth mean is carried by four
scenes: 0265, 1001, 0024, 0104), coverage, AUC/RA-AUC recomputed under one protocol.
Costless once (1) runs.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Tier 2 --- the decisive test (weeks)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=4pt,resume]
\item \textbf{Five-seed retrain with their recipe.} Training is self-supervised on
MegaDepth --- no label infrastructure needed. Tests whether the two phenomena we
document for RESfM generalize: (a) the \emph{checkpoint-provenance gap} (released vs.\
recipe-reproducible); (b) the \emph{training-seed lottery} (bands, bimodality,
catastrophic scene cells). \emph{Decision value: if either appears, the paper's
reliability methodology upgrades from ``about RESfM'' to ``about deep SfM training'' ---
the strongest possible answer to a ``VGPA supersedes this'' review. If neither appears,
VGPA earns a genuine robustness credit and the composition case strengthens.}
\item \textbf{VGPA on OUR track suite} (same npz benchmarks as every row of our tables):
MegaDepth, 1DSfM at 43\%, 1DSfM-hard at 60\%, Strecha, BlendedMVS, Olsson. Requires
adapting their view-graph construction to consume our tracks or rebuilding their
front-end on our scenes --- scope known only after reading their code. Two questions
their paper cannot answer: \emph{does the view-graph modality escape the Olsson
collapse} (and specifically the dtu106 basin --- our tail decomposition shows that one
scene is the collapse), and \emph{does their two-view front-end survive 60\%
contamination}, where pairwise geometry estimation itself degrades --- the label-ceiling
band where our label-free removal is the only working recipe.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Tier 3 --- composition (after Tiers 1--2)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=4pt,resume]
\item \textbf{VGPA-init for the collapse basin.} Use VGPA's global poses to initialize
or anchor the joint reconstruction on the catastrophic cells (dtu106; the Olsson
collapse seeds; BMVS bimodal seeds). \emph{Caution: Fadi reports VGPA+RESfM composition
underperformed --- get the configuration details from him first (which coupling, which
datasets, worse than what). Our probe differs on both axes he presumably tested:
targeted at basin-failure cells rather than benchmark-wide, and composed with the
self-supervised arms rather than supervised RESfM.}
\item \textbf{End-to-end label-free pipeline.} VGPA poses + our track-level adaptive
cleaning + robust BA: both stages self-supervised --- the composition both papers'
future-work sections gesture at. Report under both metric families (raw error over
registered + RA-AUC), since our lens-4 results show the two can invert verdicts.
\item \textbf{Retrieval-graph stress test.} Their MegaLoc@30 sparse-graph variant on
contaminated scenes: sparse graphs amplify the cost of bad edges; our contamination
axis is the natural stressor their Table 5 never meets.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.4cm}\noindent\textbf{Notes.} \emph{RESfM-scratch} is the controlled baseline (our
uniform protocol: reprojection-based checkpoint selection, margin $10^{-4}$). \emph{RESfM-faithful}
restores the authors' two released secondary values (Accuracy-based checkpoint selection, margin
$10^{-3}$); it is weaker on five of six datasets, so margins against the primary baseline are lower
bounds. \emph{RESfM-released} are the authors' published weights (MegaDepth directly comparable; OOD
on our rebuilt tracks). \emph{RESfM at $10^{-4}$, 30k} is the converged low-rate supervised variant
(strongest supervised OOD). \emph{ESFM$^{\ast}$} trains and tests on ground-truth-cleaned tracks
(clean-track reference, authors' in-place-zeroing protocol). \emph{GLOMAP} and \emph{COLMAP} run on
the same tracks with known intrinsics; GLOMAP rotations use reflection-aware alignment.
